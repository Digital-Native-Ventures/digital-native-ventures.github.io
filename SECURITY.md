# Security Policy

## Reporting Vulnerabilities

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities to `security@ntiv.io`.

Do not open public GitHub issues for suspected vulnerabilities. Include a concise description, affected repository or service, reproduction steps when safe to share, impact, and any relevant logs or screenshots with secrets removed.

## Handling

We will acknowledge credible reports as soon as practical, triage severity, and coordinate remediation before public disclosure. We may ask for additional detail to reproduce or validate the issue.

## Supported Scope

The actively maintained default branch and current production deployment are in scope. Historical branches, local development forks, and third-party services are out of scope unless the issue affects the maintained NTIV deployment.

## Safe Harbor

Good-faith research that avoids privacy violations, service disruption, data destruction, credential exposure, and persistence is welcome. Stop testing and contact us immediately if you encounter non-public data, secrets, or access beyond your own account.
